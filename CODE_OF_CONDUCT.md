# ميثاق السلوك — Code of Conduct

## التعهد

بصفتي مساهماً أو عضواً أو قائداً في مجتمع هذا المشروع، أتعهد بجعل المشاركة فيه خالية من التحرش لجميع الأطراف، بغضّ النظر عن العمر، أو حجم الجسم، أو الإعاقة الظاهرة أو غير الظاهرة، أو العرق، أو الخصائص الجنسية، أو الهوية الجندرية، أو التعبير عنها، أو مستوى الخبرة، أو التعليم، أو الحالة الاجتماعية والاقتصادية، أو الجنسية، أو المظهر الشخصي، أو العِرق، أو الدين، أو الانتماء المهني القانوني.

يتعهد القادة بتقديم القدوة للسلوك المفيد والمحترم، واتخاذ إجراءات تصحيحية مناسبة وعادلة عند حدوث سلوكيات يناقض هذا الميثاق.

> **ملاحظة خاصة بالمجتمع القانوني:** هذا المشروع يجمع محامين ومطورين ومتخصصين. خلافات مهنية حول مذاهب قانونية أو قرارات معمارية مرحّب بها بشدة — الخلاف على الفكرة لا يعني الخصومة مع صاحبها.

## The Pledge

As members, contributors, and leaders, we pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or professional affiliation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community and the Egyptian legal community this tool serves

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without explicit consent
- **Sharing or requesting real client case data in issues, chats, or PRs — never
  post confidential legal matter details; use fictional fixtures instead**
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior
that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, PRs,
chat channels) and when an individual is officially representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project team at **report@crimsys.example** (replace with the
maintainer's contact) or via [GitHub contact](https://github.com/AlySelim2001).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
reporter of any incident.
