## 💼 نسخة LinkedIn (أكثر رسمية — للمهنيين والمستثمرين التقنيين)

<div dir="ltr">

**⚖️ After months of development, we're opening CRIM-SYS 2026 to a closed group of Egyptian criminal-law practitioners.**

The problem is well known to anyone who has worked near a courtroom: litigation
files scattered across paper, personal notebooks, and WhatsApp threads — while a
single missed appeal deadline can cost an entire case.

CRIM-SYS 2026 is a native Android case-management platform built around three
non-negotiables:

**1. Offline-first.** Room as the single source of truth, with a deferred action
queue that drains to Firestore when connectivity returns. Courtrooms have poor
signal — the app was designed for that reality, not around it.

**2. On-device encryption.** A SQLCipher-encrypted database whose key is wrapped
by the Android Keystore and never leaves the device. No servers, no accounts, no
analytics. The privacy policy fits in one sentence: we collect nothing.

**3. Arabic-first UX.** Native RTL interface, Arabic type system, and legal
content (CPC 150/1950, Law 174/2025) presented as reference data — with a clear
disclaimer that every computed deadline is advisory until the responsible
attorney verifies it.

Engineering highlights: Kotlin 2.1 + Jetpack Compose (Material 3), Clean
Architecture with a strict `Result<T>` contract, Hilt DI, baseline profiles for
cold-start, R8-minified signed releases distributed via GitHub Releases with an
in-app update checker.

For lawyers: closed beta registration via WhatsApp — 01119886662.
For engineers: the repository documents the full architecture — the project
welcomes review and contribution.

#LegalTech #Android #Kotlin #JetpackCompose #Egypt #CriminalLaw #OfflineFirst
